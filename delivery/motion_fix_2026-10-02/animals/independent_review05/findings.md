# Independent source visual review

All 48 candidate_05 source clips were viewed at 11 timestamps in both original
300-square game and side cameras through all48_01–24.png. These are source
renders, not formal GLB/runtime acceptance. Candidate_06 supersedes 05. Comparing
the game-sheet byte hashes identified only Lie_To_Sit and Sit_To_Lie as changed;
their 06 game and side grids were viewed separately. The previously diagnosed
05 transition endpoint mismatch is not attributed to 06.

The reviewed source silhouettes show readable standing, looking, sitting,
lying, sleeping, sniffing, leg lifting, and side lying. Side lying prepares by
folding the legs before rolling. The side camera distinguishes Play_Bow (head
held forward/up) from Stretch_Front (head/neck lowered). Sleep places the head
near the front paws and differs from awake Lie_Idle. No gross support or knee
flip was observed in these samples; this does not certify intermediate frames
or millimetre floor clearance.

Candidate_06 supplements were also viewed: Startle 0–0.4 s, Shake_Head and
Shake_Off 0.15–0.55 s, and Scratch_Left/Right 0.9–1.3 s, each 25 timestamps at
60 Hz and two cameras. Rear oblique scratch views resolve the original
occlusion: the lifted hind paw makes small repeated movements beside the ear,
while the front paws support the sitting body. The original front/side view
alone did not establish a missing scratch.

Remaining expression notes: Startle is a mild, short head/neck alert response
with little leg/body recoil. Shake_Head reads as rapid alternating head turns;
Shake_Off has larger head/neck movement but comparatively little torso/hip
movement. Tail_Tuck_Cautious in05/06 read more as lowered tail/caution than a clear
tail tucked between both hind legs; the later revision below addresses that
observation. These are visual
readability observations, not a request to replace accepted style or proof of
runtime defects. Black overlapping leg silhouettes limit precise foot-contact
judgment. Root separately reviews the formal runtime and numerical support.

Additional source review: Husky candidate_07, Shibainu candidate_02 and Cat
candidate_02 `NG_Tail_Tuck_Cautious__side_grid.png` were viewed at all11 timestamps
in each original source grid. The revised tails lower and curl toward the inside
of the hind legs/underbelly around1.2–1.8 s; this reads as a tuck more clearly than
the earlier horizontal lowered tail. Shiba retains its breed's curled silhouette.
Cat candidate_02 `NG_Threat_Arch__side_grid.png` was also viewed at all11 timestamps.
Its angular raised back silhouette is readable, with four feet visibly supporting
the pose. This grid holds an arched posture with small movement; it does not prove
the entry/exit transitions or runtime floor contact. These four source sheets are
supplements, not a repeated review of all three formal libraries. Root separately
checks the imported runtime, while this reviewer retains the final Shiba runtime
review window.

Shiba03/Cat03 change only Stretch's kind from bow to stretch; the author records
the other47 NG arrays as identical to02. Historical02 TailTuck/arch observations
therefore still apply to those unchanged arrays, while formal GLB and source

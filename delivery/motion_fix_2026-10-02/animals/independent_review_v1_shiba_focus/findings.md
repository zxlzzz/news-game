# Shiba candidate v1 expression review

Stage-only review; this is not final runtime acceptance. Root retained v1 captures
as a failure snapshot because the separate lie recovery path had a confirmed paw
jump. No animal source/runtime data were changed by this reviewer.

All18 original-pixel review pages were viewed: shake, shake_head and startle,
each180 timestamps over2.0–4.983 s at60 Hz from0/90/270 degrees. `index.json`
preserves exact source indices/times and the original manifest path. Three
fixed-pixel detail grids were also viewed; `detail_index.json` records their
indices and crop boxes. Current Shibainu candidate_03 source game/side grids for
NG_Shake_Off, NG_Shake_Head and NG_Startle were viewed for comparison. These three
arrays are unchanged from02; author records03's only change as Stretch kind
bow→stretch. The original runtime manifest fingerprints the imported03 GLB.

- Shake: rapid alternating head/neck movement is clearly present around2.85–4.1 s,
  with small chest/back follow-through and supported legs. It is not a missed
  action or completely static pose. Body twisting remains much smaller than
  head movement, matching the current source's expression limitation.
- Shake head: head-only alternating turns are clearly visible, especially in
  the front detail sequence3.067–3.317 s. Body stays more stable than shake;
  its localized expression survives the runtime connection.
- Startle: short head/neck alert lift, a small foreleg/body reaction, then return
  to support are visible around2.67–2.92 s. It remains a mild response, rather
  than a strong jump or whole-body recoil. The current source grid has the same
  mild alert silhouette.

No complete loss of these three source expressions was observed. Black overlap,
small source silhouette and frontal legs limit fine skin/foot-contact judgment.
This expression check does not cover lie enter/out, floor millimetres, later
runtime revisions, the remaining17 Shiba actions or normal restoration paths.

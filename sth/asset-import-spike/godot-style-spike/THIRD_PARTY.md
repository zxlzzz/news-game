# Third-party inputs

This visual spike uses two models from Quaternius Standard FREE packs:

- `Building_Small_1.glb` — Downtown City MegaKit Standard
- `CommonTree_1.glb` — Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard

Both local source packages include `License_Standard.txt` declaring the models
under CC0 1.0 Universal. Original source: <https://quaternius.com/>.

The edge-detection structure in `grayscale_outline.gdshader` is adapted from
Conlan2's CC0 “Chants of Sennaar Style Shader”:
<https://godotshaders.com/shader/chants-of-sennaar-style-shader/>.
